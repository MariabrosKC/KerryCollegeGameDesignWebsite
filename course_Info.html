Overview

Course Aim

The global games industry is estimated to be worth in excess of €66 billion — and Ireland is part of it, with international publishers, middleware companies and a growing number of independent studios based here.

This course is aimed at those who wish to explore the opportunities in that rapidly expanding industry. You will design and produce your own games using industry-standard games engines, learning the creative and technical skills a studio expects from a graduate entrant.

Everything you make becomes part of a working portfolio — the single most important thing you will bring to an interview, an application or a third-level interview panel.

What you will do

  • Build and publish your own playable games
  • Model, animate and light 3D assets from scratch
  • Design game mechanics, levels and interfaces
  • Work in a studio-style, project-led environment
  • Prepare a CV and portfolio for industry or further study

Course Content

Eight QQI Level 5 modules, balanced between creative design, technical production and the professional skills employers ask for. Module codes shown are the QQI award codes.

5N0690

Communications

Written, spoken and digital communication skills for the studio and the workplace.

5N1603

Computer 3D Modelling & Animation

Model, texture, light and animate the 3D assets that make up a game world.

5N1929

Computer Illustrated Graphics

Digital illustration and graphics production using industry software.

5N0784

Design Skills

Design principles, idea generation and visual problem-solving from brief to finish.

5N1438

Digital Editing

Edit video and audio, and prepare media assets ready for a game build.

5N1651

Games Analysis Design

Deconstruct what makes games work — mechanics, genres, pacing and player feedback.

5N2985

Personal Professional Development

Career planning, CV writing, portfolio presentation and interview preparation.

5N1910

Web Authoring

Build and publish responsive websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Entry

Requirements

Applicants are considered on the information supplied at application stage. Places are offered subject to interview and to places being available.

  • Age 16+

    Applicants must be 16 years of age or over on entry to the course.

  • Leaving Certificate or equivalent

    Leaving Cert, LCVP, LCA or a QQI Level 4 Major Award.

  • Competent English

    Written and spoken English at CEFR B2 level or higher.

  • Permission to work in Ireland

    Required for non-EU/EEA applicants.

23+

Mature applicant route

A mature applicant route is available for applicants aged 23 and over. Prior qualifications are not the deciding factor — bring your interest in games, your motivation, and any creative or technical work you have done.

Next steps

Where It Can Take You

A QQI Level 5 award is a recognised stepping stone — into further study or straight into creative and technical work in the games and wider digital media sector.

Further Study

Progress to a QQI Level 6 award, a Higher Certificate or a degree in games design, animation or computing.

Games Studios

Entry-level roles and internships across QA, level design, 3D asset production and game design.

Creative Media

Skills that transfer directly to animation, VFX, motion graphics, video editing and illustration.

Indie Development

Ship your own games independently using the engines, pipeline and portfolio built on the course.
